## "Find your ride" quiz (on the homepage)

- Four quick questions (daily distance, main purpose, what matters most, preferred design) give a personalised design suggestion.
- The distance options in the quiz ("Up to 20 km", "Between 20 and 50 km", "More than 50 km") describe the RIDER'S typical daily ride. They are NOT the scooter's range.
- The website says the suggestion "is based on your preferences, not verified range or performance data. Confirm technical specifications with RENET before purchase."

## Contact and enquiry process

- Email: info@renet.in
- Homepage contact form (section "Start a conversation"): fields are Name (required), Email (required), Phone number (optional), Model of interest (D-8 White, D-8 Black or General enquiry) and Message. Enquiries are sent to info@renet.in and the RENET team follows up about the model, availability and the customer's questions. Submitting means the customer agrees RENET may contact them about the enquiry.
- Each product page has an "Enquire about D-8 White/Black" button that opens an email to info@renet.in.
- Test ride / call-back request form: on the homepage contact section ("Book a test ride" tab) and at the bottom of each product page. Customers give their name, mobile number, city, preferred model and preferred contact method (phone call, WhatsApp or email). The RENET team then contacts them; test ride availability, timing and location are confirmed by the team.
- WhatsApp: +91 89294 01385 — customers can tap the WhatsApp button at the bottom-left of any page to start a chat with RENET.
- No address or social media accounts are published on the website.

## Compare and running-cost tools (on the homepage)

- "Compare" section: D-8 White and D-8 Black side by side. Confirmed rows: design character, light signature, colour, type, market. Range, top speed, motor, battery capacity, charging time, warranty and price show "Awaiting confirmation".
- Running-cost estimator: visitors enter their daily distance, riding days, an energy-use assumption (Wh/km), electricity tariff, charging losses and, optionally, their petrol vehicle's mileage and petrol price. Results are estimates of energy cost only, based on the visitor's inputs. The default energy-use value is a general assumption for small electric scooters, NOT an official D-8 figure — never quote calculator outputs as RENET specifications or guaranteed savings.
